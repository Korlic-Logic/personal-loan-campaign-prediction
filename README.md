# Personal Loan Campaign Prediction

Classification project predicting which depositor customers of AllLife Bank are likely to take out a personal loan.

## Business Context

AllLife Bank is a US bank with a growing customer base. Most customers are liability customers (depositors), while the number of asset customers (borrowers) is small. The bank wants to grow its loan business among existing depositors and convert more liability customers into personal-loan customers. A previous campaign had a healthy conversion rate, and the bank wants to run more targeted, cost-effective campaigns going forward.

## Objective

Predict whether a liability customer will buy a personal loan, understand which customer attributes most drive that purchase, and identify which customer segments to target.
